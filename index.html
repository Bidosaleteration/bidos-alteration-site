ABOUT US

Sixteen Years of Craftsmanship in North Miami

Bidos Alteration has been a cornerstone of North Miami for over 16 years. Founded in 2010, our business built its reputation on precision, reliability, and exceptional customer care. We are known for respecting deadlines, delivering high quality work, and treating every garment with the attention it deserves.

Our clients trust us because we combine craftsmanship with modern convenience: online booking, secure payment, automated reminders, and home delivery.

To make every customer feel confident, comfortable, and stylish in perfectly fitted clothing.
